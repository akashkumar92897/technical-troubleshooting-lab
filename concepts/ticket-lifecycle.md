# Ticket Lifecycle

## What is a Ticket?

A ticket is a trackable record of a user's technical issue or service request within a support system.

It allows a support team to record, assign, investigate, update, resolve, and close a case while maintaining a history of the actions taken.

A typical ticket may contain:

- Ticket ID
- User or customer information
- Issue title
- Problem description
- Category
- Priority
- Status
- Assigned support agent
- Investigation notes
- Customer communication
- Resolution details
- Created and updated timestamps

---

## Why Ticketing is Important

Ticketing helps support teams manage customer issues systematically instead of relying on informal communication or memory.

A ticket provides a central record of:

- What the problem is
- Who is affected
- How urgent the issue is
- Who is responsible for handling it
- What troubleshooting has already been performed
- What resolution was applied
- Whether the issue needs escalation

Good ticket documentation also allows another support agent to continue working on the case without repeating the entire investigation.
