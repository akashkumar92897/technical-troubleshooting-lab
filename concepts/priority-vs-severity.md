# Priority vs Severity

## Overview

Priority and severity are both used in IT support and incident management to help teams determine how an issue should be handled.

Although they are related, they describe different aspects of an incident.

- **Severity** describes the impact of an issue.
- **Priority** describes how urgently the issue should be handled.

The exact definitions and levels can vary between organizations.

---

## What is Severity?

**Severity** describes the degree of impact caused by an incident.

It considers factors such as:

- Number of users affected
- Business impact
- Availability of a critical service
- Loss of important functionality
- Operational disruption

### Example

An application outage affecting 5,000 users would generally have a much higher severity than a minor display problem affecting one user.

---

## What is Priority?

**Priority** determines how urgently a support team should respond to and work on an issue.

Priority can depend on:

- Business impact
- Number of users affected
- Severity
- Business criticality
- Customer importance
- Available workarounds
- Service-level requirements

### Example

An issue affecting a critical business application may receive a high priority because delaying its resolution could significantly affect operations.
