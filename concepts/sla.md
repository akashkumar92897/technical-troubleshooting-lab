# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## Overview

A Service Level Agreement (SLA) is an agreed level of service between a service provider and a customer or organization.

In IT support, an SLA can define expectations such as:

- Response time
- Resolution targets
- Service availability
- Support hours
- Escalation requirements

The exact SLA terms depend on the organization, service, and customer agreement.

---

## SLA in IT Support

Support teams use SLAs to establish how quickly different types of issues should receive attention.

For example, an organization may define different response targets based on ticket priority:

       ↓
Fastest Response Target
