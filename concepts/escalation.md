# Technical Support Escalation

## Overview

Escalation is the process of transferring a support issue to another support level, specialized team, or appropriate technical resource when the issue cannot be resolved within the current support scope.

Escalation is a normal part of technical support. A support agent is not expected to solve every issue independently.

The goal of escalation is to ensure that the issue reaches the appropriate team with enough information to continue the investigation efficiently.

---

## When Should an Issue Be Escalated?

An issue may need to be escalated when:

- It requires technical expertise beyond the current support level.
- The support agent does not have the required permissions or access.
- The issue requires changes that only another team can perform.
- The problem appears to be related to an application, server, database, or infrastructure component outside the agent's scope.
- Multiple users or customers are affected.
- The issue may represent a larger service outage.
- Troubleshooting within the current support scope has not resolved the issue.
- The issue requires specialized investigation.

The exact escalation rules depend on the organization's support procedures.

---

## Common Escalation Levels

Organizations may use different support structures, but a common model is:
