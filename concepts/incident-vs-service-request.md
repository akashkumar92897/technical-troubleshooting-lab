# Incident vs Service Request

## Overview

IT support teams handle different types of user interactions. Two common categories are **incidents** and **service requests**.

Understanding the difference helps support teams correctly categorize tickets, determine the appropriate workflow, and assign the right level of priority.

---

## What is an Incident?

An **incident** is an unplanned interruption, degradation, or failure of a service or system.

In simple terms:

> Something that was expected to work is not working correctly.

### Examples

- A user cannot log in to an application.
- A company website is unavailable.
- A computer cannot connect to the network.
- An application suddenly crashes.
- A previously working feature stops functioning.

The primary goal of incident management is to **restore normal service as quickly and safely as possible**.

---

## What is a Service Request?

A **service request** is a user's request for a standard service, access, information, or approved action.

In simple terms:

> The user needs something rather than reporting something that is broken.

### Examples

- Requesting access to an application
- Requesting installation of approved software
- Requesting a password reset
- Requesting information or documentation
- Requesting a standard account or service

The exact classification of a request can vary between organizations and their support processes.
